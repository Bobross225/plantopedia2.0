Watermelon

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Late spring when soil is 70°F+.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Full sun with long growing season.

  3. Prepare Soil: Sandy, well-drained, fertile soil; pH 6.0–6.8.

  4. Space Plants Properly: 3–5 feet apart in rows 6–8 feet apart.

  5. Water Deeply: 1–2 inches weekly; reduce watering before harvest.

  6. Harvest When Mature: Bottom turns yellow, stem starts to dry.
