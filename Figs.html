Figs

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Best in spring or early fall in warm climates.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Needs 6–8 hrs of full sun daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Well-drained soil; tolerates sandy or loamy soils, pH 6.0–6.5.

  4. Space Trees Properly: Plant 15–20 feet apart.

  5. Water Deeply: 1–1.5 inches weekly in dry periods, less once established.

  6. Harvest Ripe: Pick figs when soft, drooping, and fully colored.
