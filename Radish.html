Radish

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Early spring or late summer for cooler weather.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Full sun or partial shade.

  3. Prepare Soil: Loose, fertile, well-drained soil; pH 6.0–7.0.

  4. Sow Seeds Properly: ½ inch deep, thin to 1–2 inches apart.

  5. Water Consistently: 1 inch per week; keep soil moist for fast growth.

  6. Harvest on Time: Pick when roots reach desired size; delays make them woody.
