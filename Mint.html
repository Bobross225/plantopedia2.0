Mint

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Spring after frost or fall in warm climates.

  2. Pick a Spot: Prefers partial shade but tolerates full sun.

  3. Prepare Soil: Moist, rich soil with compost; pH 6.0–7.0.

  4. Contain Growth: Plant in pots or with barriers to control spreading.

  5. Water Regularly: 1 inch weekly; keep soil consistently moist.

  6. Harvest Often: Snip leaves regularly to encourage bushy growth.
