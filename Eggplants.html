Eggplants

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Transplant after frost, when soil is 70°F+.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Needs 6–8 hrs of direct sun daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Fertile, well-drained soil enriched with compost; pH 6.0–6.8.

  4. Space Plants Properly: 18–24 inches apart in rows 30 inches apart.

  5. Water Consistently: 1–1.5 inches weekly; mulch to retain soil moisture.

  6. Harvest Carefully: Cut fruits when glossy, firm, and 6–8 inches long.
