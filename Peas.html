Peas

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Early spring or late fall in cool weather.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: 6+ hours of sunlight daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Loamy, well-drained soil; pH 6.0–7.5.

  4. Space Plants Properly: Sow seeds 1 inch deep, 2 inches apart.

  5. Water Consistently: About 1 inch weekly, keeping soil moist.

  6. Harvest on Time: Pick pods young and tender for best flavor.
