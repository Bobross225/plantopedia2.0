Sweet Potatoes

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Spring after soil warms to 70°F+.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Full sun for 6–8 hours daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Light, well-drained sandy soil; pH 5.5–6.5.

  4. Plant Slips Properly: 12–18 inches apart in rows 3 feet apart.

  5. Water Consistently: 1–2 inches weekly; avoid wetting foliage.

  6. Harvest at Peak: 90–120 days after planting; cure before storage.
