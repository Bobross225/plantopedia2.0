About Us

Here at Plantopedia, we've come to notice that in recent years, gardens have become increasingly popular, growing produce for the community's and personal benefit.

However, in many regions throughout Alabama, plants thrive differently depending on many variables.

Here we have listed the many differences between different regions and how to grow plants within those conditions to keep gardens from failing.

The significance behind this database is the fact that many gardens are dying, and most gardens fail because people don't know exactly what plants need to grow well in their area.

Fixing this problem helps gardens succeed and makes it easier for people to grow healthy plants.

The people who benefit from this solution are those who grow gardens at home, in schools, or in the community. They'll have better tools and information to grow strong, healthy plants.

Home garden growers are our main target audience.
