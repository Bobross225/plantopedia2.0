Rutabaga

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Early spring or late summer for cool weather.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Full sun preferred.

  3. Prepare Soil: Loose, fertile, well-drained soil; pH 6.0–7.0.

  4. Sow Seeds Properly: ½ inch deep, thin to 4–6 inches apart.

  5. Water Consistently: About 1 inch per week; keep soil moist.

  6. Harvest on Time: Pull roots when 3–5 inches in diameter.
