Blackberries

  1. Plant at the Right Time: In Alabama, set blackberry plants in late winter to early spring when soil is workable.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Full sun (6–8 hours daily) is essential for best fruit production.

  3. Prepare Well-Draining Soil: Loamy soil rich in organic matter; ideal pH 5.5–6.5.

  4. Plant & Space Properly: Space plants 3–5 feet apart in rows 8–10 feet apart; add trellises for trailing varieties.

  5. Water Regularly: Provide 1–2 inches of water per week, especially during bloom and fruiting.

  6. Prune & Harvest: Prune spent canes after fruiting; harvest berries when fully black, plump, and glossy.
