Thyme



  1. Plant at the Right Time: Spring after frost danger has passed.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Full sun, at least 6 hours daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Light, well-drained soil; pH 6.0–8.0.

  4. Space Plants Properly: 12–18 inches apart.

  5. Water Sparingly: About ½ inch weekly; prefers dry soil.

  6. Harvest Often: Snip sprigs regularly to encourage growth.
