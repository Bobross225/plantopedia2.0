Blueberries

  1. Plant at the Right Time: In Alabama, plant blueberries in late winter to early spring while dormant.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Full sun (6–8 hours daily) ensures best berry size and sweetness.

  3. Prepare Acidic Soil: Blueberries need acidic soil, pH 4.5–5.5; amend with peat moss or pine needles.

  4. Plant & Space Properly: Space bushes 4–5 feet apart in rows 8–10 feet apart; set roots slightly deeper than in pots.

  5. Water Consistently: Provide 1–2 inches of water per week, especially during fruit set and ripening.

  6. Mulch & Maintain: Mulch 2–4 inches with pine bark or needles; prune lightly after harvest to shape and renew growth.
