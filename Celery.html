Celery

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Set transplants in early spring or fall when temps are 55–70°F.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Requires 6+ hours of full sun daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Rich, moisture-retentive soil with compost; pH 6.0–7.0.

  4. Space Plants Properly: 8–10 inches apart in rows 18–24 inches apart.

  5. Water Frequently: Needs 1–1.5 inches of water weekly; never let soil dry out.

  6. Harvest Carefully: Cut stalks at soil level when they're 8–12 inches tall and crisp.
