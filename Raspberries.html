Raspberry

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Early spring while dormant.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: 6–8 hours of sunlight daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Well-drained, fertile soil; pH 5.5–6.5.

  4. Provide Support: Use trellises for upright varieties.

  5. Water Regularly: 1–1.5 inches weekly; keep soil moist but not soggy.

  6. Harvest Carefully: Pick berries when fully colored and easy to detach.
