Turnips

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Early spring or late summer for cool weather.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Full sun or partial shade.

  3. Prepare Soil: Loose, fertile, well-drained soil; pH 6.0–7.5.

  4. Sow Seeds Properly: ½ inch deep, thin to 3–4 inches apart.

  5. Water Consistently: 1 inch per week; avoid drying out.

  6. Harvest When Ready: Roots 2–3 inches across; greens can be picked anytime.
