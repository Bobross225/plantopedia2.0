Tomatoes

  1. Plant at the Right Time: After last frost when soil is 65°F+.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: 6–8 hours of sunlight daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Rich, well-drained soil with compost; pH 6.0–6.8.

  4. Space Plants Properly: 18–24 inches apart in rows 3 feet apart.

  5. Water Regularly: 1–2 inches per week; water at base to prevent disease.

  6. Harvest When Ripe: Fruits fully colored and slightly firm.
