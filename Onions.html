Onions

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Early spring or fall depending on variety.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Needs 6–8 hrs of direct sunlight daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Loose, sandy, well-drained soil; pH 6.0–7.0.

  4. Space Plants Properly: 4–6 inches apart in rows 12–18 inches apart.

  5. Water Consistently: 1 inch weekly; reduce watering as bulbs mature.

  6. Harvest at Peak: Pull when tops fall over and turn yellow-brown.
