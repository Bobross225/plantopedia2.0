Carrot

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Sow seeds in early spring or fall when temps are 55–75°F.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Full sun (6–8 hrs), though light shade is tolerated.

  3. Prepare Soil: Loose, sandy loam at least 12 inches deep, free of rocks.

  4. Sow Seeds Properly: ¼ inch deep; thin seedlings to 2–3 inches apart.

  5. Water Regularly: 1 inch per week, keeping soil evenly moist to prevent cracking.

  6. Harvest Carefully: Pull when roots are bright orange and ½–1 inch thick.
