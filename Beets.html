Beets

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Sow beets in early spring or late summer when soil temps are 50–75°F.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Full sun (6+ hours daily) is best, though beets tolerate partial shade.

  3. Prepare Loose Soil: Use sandy or loamy, well-drained soil; work in compost; pH 6.0–7.5.

  4. Sow Seeds Properly: Plant seeds ½ inch deep, 1–2 inches apart; thin seedlings to 3–4 inches apart for root growth.

  5. Water Consistently: Provide about 1 inch of water per week; keep soil evenly moist to avoid woody roots.

  6. Harvest on Time: Pull beets when roots are 1.5–3 inches in diameter; harvest greens anytime when young and tender.
