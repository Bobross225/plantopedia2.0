Bananas

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Set banana plants outdoors in late spring once soil and air temperatures stay above 65°F.

  2. Pick a Sunny, Warm Spot: Bananas need full sun (8+ hours daily) and protection from strong winds.

  3. Prepare Rich Soil: Use fertile, well-draining soil mixed with compost; ideal pH is 5.5–7.0.

  4. Plant & Space Properly: Space plants 8–10 feet apart; dig wide holes to accommodate roots.

  5. Water Generously: Provide 1–2 inches of water weekly; bananas like evenly moist soil but not standing water.

  6. Feed & Maintain: Fertilize monthly with a balanced or high-potassium fertilizer; remove suckers so one main stem produces fruit.
