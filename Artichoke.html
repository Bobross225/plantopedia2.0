Artichokes

  1. Plant at the Right Time: In mild-winter areas plant in fall (zones 8–11). In colder regions start in spring and grow as an annual.

  2. Pick a Sunny, Sheltered Spot: Full sun (6–8 hours) with some wind protection; avoid sites with hard late frosts.

  3. Prepare Rich, Well-Draining Soil: Amend with compost; aim for loamy soil, pH about 6.5–7.5; use raised beds if drainage is poor.

  4. Plant & Space Properly: Plant crowns/divisions at soil level and space plants about 3–4 feet apart to allow mature growth.

  5. Water & Mulch: Water deeply to keep soil consistently moist — about 1–2 inches per week, more in hot/dry spells; mulch heavily for winter protection and moisture retention.

  6. Feed, Harvest & Prune: Feed monthly with organic fertilizer, harvest tight buds ≥3 inches, and prune back spent growth to encourage fresh shoots.
