Cucumber

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Sow seeds outdoors once soil is 65°F+.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Full sun, 6–8 hrs daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Rich, moist, well-drained soil; pH 6.0–6.8.

  4. Space Plants Properly: 12–18 inches apart; provide trellis support if vining.

  5. Water Regularly: 1–2 inches weekly; keep soil evenly moist for crisp fruits.

  6. Harvest Often: Pick when 6–8 inches long to encourage continuous production.
