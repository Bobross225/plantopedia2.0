Peaches

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Late winter or early spring while dormant.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Needs 6–8 hours of sun daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Well-drained sandy loam, pH 6.0–6.5.

  4. Space Trees Properly: 15–20 feet apart.

  5. Water Weekly: 1 inch weekly, especially in dry spells.

  6. Prune & Harvest: Prune in late winter; harvest when fruit is firm but fragrant.
