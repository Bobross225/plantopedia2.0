Strawberry

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Early spring for best growth.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Full sun for 6–8 hours daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Well-drained, fertile soil; pH 5.5–6.5.

  4. Space Plants Properly: 12–18 inches apart.

  5. Water Consistently: 1 inch per week; keep soil moist.

  6. Harvest Often: Pick berries when bright red and firm.
