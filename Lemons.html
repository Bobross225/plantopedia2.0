Lemons

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Best in spring after last frost.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Requires 8+ hrs of direct sunlight.

  3. Prepare Soil: Well-draining sandy or loamy soil; pH 5.5–6.5.

  4. Water Regularly: 1–2 inches weekly; keep soil evenly moist.

  5. Fertilize Often: Every 4–6 weeks with citrus-specific fertilizer.

  6. Harvest at Peak: Pick when lemons are fully yellow and slightly soft.
