Lettuce

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Spring and fall, prefers 55–70°F.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: 6 hrs of sun daily; tolerates partial shade.

  3. Prepare Soil: Loose, fertile, moist soil; pH 6.0–7.0.

  4. Space Plants Properly: 6–12 inches apart depending on variety.

  5. Water Regularly: 1–1.5 inches weekly; soil should stay moist.

  6. Harvest Often: Pick outer leaves or cut whole heads when mature.
