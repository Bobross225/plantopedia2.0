Okra

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Sow in late spring when soil warms to 70°F+.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Full sun for at least 6–8 hours daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Well-drained, fertile soil with compost; pH 6.0–6.8.

  4. Space Plants Properly: 12–18 inches apart in rows 3 feet apart.

  5. Water Consistently: 1 inch weekly; more during hot, dry spells.

  6. Harvest Often: Pick pods when 2–4 inches long to keep plants producing.
