Sugarcane

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Spring after frost; soil at least 70°F.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Full sun for optimal growth.

  3. Prepare Soil: Fertile, well-drained soil with organic matter.

  4. Plant Cuttings Properly: 12–18 inches apart, 4–6 inches deep.

  5. Water Regularly: 1–2 inches per week; more during dry spells.

  6. Harvest When Mature: 10–12 months after planting or when stalks are firm.
