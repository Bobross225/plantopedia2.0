Cantaloupe

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Sow seeds in late spring when soil is at least 70°F.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Needs 8+ hours of direct sunlight daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Sandy, well-drained soil rich in organic matter; pH 6.0–6.8.

  4. Space Plants Properly: 36–42 inches apart on raised hills or mounds.

  5. Water Consistently: 1–2 inches weekly until fruit sets, then reduce for sweeter melons.

  6. Harvest at Peak: Pick when rind turns yellow-beige and fruit slips easily from the stem.
