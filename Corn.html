Corn

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Sow seeds outdoors after frost, when soil is 60°F+.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Needs full sun (8+ hrs daily).

  3. Prepare Soil: Fertile, well-drained soil; pH 6.0–6.8.

  4. Plant in Blocks: Plant in short blocks (not long rows) for better pollination.

  5. Water Deeply: 1–1.5 inches weekly, especially during tasseling and ear filling.

  6. Harvest at Peak: Pick when kernels are plump, milky, and silk turns brown.
