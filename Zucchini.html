Zucchini

  1. Plant at the Right Time: After last frost when soil is 65°F+.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: 6–8 hours of sun daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Fertile, well-drained soil; pH 6.0–7.5.

  4. Space Plants Properly: 2–3 feet apart in rows 3–4 feet apart.

  5. Water Consistently: 1–1.5 inches weekly; water at base.

  6. Harvest When Young: Pick fruits 6–8 inches long for best flavor.
