Oregano

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Sow in spring after frost danger passes.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Needs 6+ hours of sunlight daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Light, well-drained soil; pH 6.0–8.0.

  4. Space Plants Properly: 12–18 inches apart.

  5. Water Lightly: 1 inch every 7–10 days; avoid soggy roots.

  6. Harvest Regularly: Pinch stems to encourage bushy growth.
