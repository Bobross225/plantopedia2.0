Pumpkins

  1. Plant at the Right Time: After last frost when soil reaches 70°F.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Full sun for 6–8 hours daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Fertile, well-drained soil with compost; pH 6.0–6.8.

  4. Space Plants Properly: 3–4 feet apart in rows 6–8 feet apart.

  5. Water Regularly: 1–2 inches per week; avoid wetting leaves to reduce disease.

  6. Harvest When Mature: Pick when skin is hard and fully colored.
