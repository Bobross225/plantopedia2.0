Spinach

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Early spring or fall in cool weather.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Partial to full sun.

  3. Prepare Soil: Loose, fertile, well-drained soil; pH 6.0–7.0.

  4. Sow Seeds Properly: ½ inch deep, thin to 2–3 inches apart.

  5. Water Consistently: 1 inch per week; keep soil moist.

  6. Harvest Leaves Often: Pick outer leaves to encourage new growth.
