Brussels Sprouts

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Start seeds in late summer for fall harvest in Alabama's mild winters.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: 6+ hours of full sun daily for strong stalk growth.

  3. Prepare Soil: Rich, well-drained soil with pH 6.0–7.5; mix in compost for nutrients.

  4. Space Plants Properly: Set transplants 18–24 inches apart in rows 30 inches apart.

  5. Water Regularly: Provide 1–1.5 inches of water per week, keeping soil evenly moist.

  6. Harvest Gradually: Twist off sprouts from the bottom up when firm and 1–2 inches in diameter.
