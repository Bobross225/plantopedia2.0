Asparagus

  1. Plant at the Right Time: In Alabama, plant crowns in late winter to early spring when soil can be worked.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Full sun (6–8 hours) is best, though asparagus tolerates light shade.

  3. Prepare Deep, Fertile Soil: Work compost into loose, well-drained soil; asparagus prefers pH 6.5–7.5.

  4. Plant & Space Crowns: Dig trenches 8–10 inches deep, place crowns 12–18 inches apart, and cover with 2 inches of soil at first, gradually filling in as shoots grow.

  5. Water & Mulch: Provide about 1–2 inches of water weekly, keeping soil moist but not soggy; mulch to suppress weeds and retain moisture.

  6. Maintain & Harvest: Do not harvest the first year; light harvest in year 2; by year 3, harvest spears 6–8 inches tall, then allow ferns to grow for plant health.
