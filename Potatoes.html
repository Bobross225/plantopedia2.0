Potatoes

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Early spring after last frost.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Full sun with at least 6 hours daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Loose, well-drained, fertile soil; pH 5.5–6.5.

  4. Plant Seed Potatoes: Cut into pieces with 1–2 eyes each, 4 inches deep.

  5. Water Consistently: 1–2 inches weekly; keep soil evenly moist.

  6. Harvest Carefully: Dig when plants yellow and die back; cure before storage.
