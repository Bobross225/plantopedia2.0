Legume

  1. Plant at the Right Time: After frost when soil warms to 60°F+.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: At least 6–8 hrs of full sun daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Light, well-drained, moderately fertile soil; pH 6.0–7.0.

  4. Space Plants Properly: Follow spacing for each legume (beans, peas, lentils).

  5. Water Moderately: About 1 inch weekly; avoid overwatering to prevent rot.

  6. Harvest at Peak: Pick when pods are full but not tough; promotes continued growth.
