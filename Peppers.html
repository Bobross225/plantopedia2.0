Peppers

  1. Plant at the Right Time: After last frost when soil reaches 70°F.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: 6–8 hours of direct sunlight daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Rich, well-drained soil; pH 6.2–7.0.

  4. Space Plants Properly: 18–24 inches apart in rows 2–3 feet apart.

  5. Water Regularly: 1–1.5 inches per week; keep soil evenly moist.

  6. Harvest Carefully: Pick peppers when firm and fully colored.
