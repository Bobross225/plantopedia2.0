There are six major soil regions within Alabama, each with its own planting needs.

Limestone Valleys & Uplands:
Soils developed from weathered limestone in river valleys (Tennessee, Coosa) and uplands.
Typically red clayey with silt‑loam surfaces (e.g. Decatur, Dewey, Bodine, Fullerton series).
Drainage is generally good; topography varies from level to rolling.

Appalachian Plateau:
Located in Northeast Alabama.
