Kale

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Spring or fall, prefers cool temps 60–70°F.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Needs 6+ hrs of sun; tolerates partial shade.

  3. Prepare Soil: Fertile, moist, well-drained soil; pH 6.0–7.5.

  4. Space Plants Properly: 12–18 inches apart in rows 18–24 inches apart.

  5. Water Regularly: 1–1.5 inches weekly; mulch to keep soil cool and moist.

  6. Harvest Carefully: Pick outer leaves when 8–10 inches long, leave center growing.
