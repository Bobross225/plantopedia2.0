Grapes

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Early spring while dormant.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Needs 7–8 hrs of full sun daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Well-drained loamy soil; pH 5.5–6.5.

  4. Provide Support: Train vines on trellises or fences for airflow.

  5. Water Deeply: 1 inch weekly during the growing season; less once established.

  6. Harvest at Peak: Pick when grapes are plump, sweet, and fully colored.
