Arugula

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Early spring or fall when temps are 50–70°F; avoid hot summer months.


  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Full sun (6+ hours) or partial shade in hotter months.


  3. Prep the Soil: Loamy, well-draining soil with organic matter; pH 6.0–7.0.


  4. Sow Seeds Properly: ¼ inch deep; thin seedlings to 3–4 inches apart.


  5. Water Regularly: About 1 inch per week, keeping soil consistently moist; supplement rainfall during dry spells.


  6. Harvest Often: Pick leaves 3–4 inches long; encourages new growth and extends the harvest.
