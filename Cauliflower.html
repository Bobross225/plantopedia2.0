Cauliflower

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Best grown in fall or early spring in Alabama's cooler months.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Needs 6+ hours of sunlight daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Rich, moist, well-drained soil; pH 6.5–7.5.

  4. Space Plants Properly: 18–24 inches apart in rows 30 inches apart.

  5. Water Consistently: 1–1.5 inches per week; don't let soil dry out.

  6. Harvest at Peak: Cut heads when firm, white, and about 6–8 inches across.
