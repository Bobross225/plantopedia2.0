Green Beans

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Sow outdoors after frost, soil 60°F+.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Needs 6–8 hrs of sun daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Light, well-drained soil enriched with compost; pH 6.0–6.8.

  4. Space Plants Properly: Bush beans 2–4 inches apart; pole beans need trellises.

  5. Water Regularly: 1–1.5 inches weekly; avoid wetting leaves to reduce disease.

  6. Harvest Often: Pick when pods are firm and snap easily, before seeds bulge.
