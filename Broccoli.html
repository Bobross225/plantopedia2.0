Broccoli

  1. Plant at the Right Time: In Alabama, sow broccoli in late summer for fall crops or late winter for spring harvest.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: Full sun (6+ hours daily) helps form large, firm heads.

  3. Prepare Soil: Rich, loamy soil with pH 6.0–7.0; add compost for nutrients.

  4. Space Plants Properly: Transplant seedlings 18 inches apart in rows 24 inches apart.

  5. Water Regularly: Give 1–1.5 inches of water weekly; keep soil evenly moist for steady growth.

  6. Harvest at the Right Time: Cut main heads when tight and firm; leave side shoots to extend harvest.
