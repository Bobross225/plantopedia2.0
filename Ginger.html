Ginger

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Start in spring once soil warms to 70°F+.

  2. Pick a Shady Spot: Prefers partial shade with indirect sunlight.

  3. Prepare Soil: Rich, loose, well-drained soil with compost; pH 5.5–6.5.

  4. Plant Rhizomes: Place pieces 2–4 inches deep, buds facing upward.

  5. Water Consistently: 1 inch weekly, keep soil evenly moist but not soggy.

  6. Harvest at Peak: Dig rhizomes 8–10 months after planting, when leaves yellow.
