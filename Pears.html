Pears

  1. Plant at the Right Time: Early spring or late fall while dormant.

  2. Pick a Sunny Spot: 6–8 hours of direct sunlight daily.

  3. Prepare Soil: Fertile, well-draining loam; pH 6.0–7.0.

  4. Space Trees Properly: 15–20 feet apart for full-sized trees.

  5. Water Weekly: 1 inch per week, especially during fruit development.

  6. Harvest at the Right Stage: Pick when firm, let ripen indoors.
